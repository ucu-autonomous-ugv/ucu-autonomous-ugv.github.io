Perception and navigation for unmanned ground vehicles

The UCU Autonomous UGV Club builds SLAM, visual navigation and open datasets for vehicles that drive in low light and off-road terrain

Explore research Reach us

Who we are

Students and faculty of UCU APPS in Lviv. We take methods from papers onto a real vehicle and share what we learn as theses, datasets and lectures.

  • Real vehicles, real terrain

    Perception and navigation built on data we record with a Clearpath Husky A200, on campus and in Stryiskyi Park

  • Algorithms first

    Computer vision and learning-based methods for SLAM, localization and obstacle avoidance

  • Open to collaboration

    Joint research, thesis supervision and industry partnerships
