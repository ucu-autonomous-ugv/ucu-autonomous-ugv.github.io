An overview of visual SLAM with a focus on making it work when the camera can barely see. Topics covered:

- Probabilistic SLAM formulation
- Keypoint-based vs. direct visual methods
- Feature extraction & tracking in low light
- Noise, exposure & sensor limitations at night
- Handling dynamic environments
